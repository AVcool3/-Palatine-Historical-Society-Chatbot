# Palatine — Notable Events, Landmarks & People

**Source:** Compiled from public reference material via web search.

## Roads & railroads

The Illinois and Wisconsin Railroad reached the Deer Grove Trading Post area in
1853; rail service reached Palatine on June 10, 1855. The line later became part
of the **Chicago and North Western Railway**, and the village grew around its
station. The historic downtown depot area remains central to Palatine's
identity. (See the Historical Society's "Roads & Railroads" history page.)

## The village seal controversy (1990s)

During the early 1990s, Palatine — along with neighboring Rolling Meadows and
the far northern suburb of Zion — was sued by atheist activist **Rob Sherman**
over its village seal and seal-defaced flag, which featured a Christian cross.
A 1992 advisory referendum to keep the seal passed, but a separate referendum to
use public funds to defend the seal failed, leading the Village to drop the seal.

## The Brown's Chicken massacre (1993)

The **Brown's Chicken massacre** was a mass murder that occurred on
**January 8, 1993**, in Palatine, when two robbers shot and killed **seven
employees** at a Brown's Chicken fast-food restaurant. The case remained
unsolved for more than nine years. In 2002 one of the assailants was implicated
by his girlfriend, and police used DNA from the scene to match a suspect,
**Juan Luna**. Luna was tried in 2007, found guilty of seven counts of
first-degree murder, and sentenced to life imprisonment. A second man, James
Degorski, was also convicted. The case is one of the most notorious crimes in
suburban Chicago history.

## Geography and growth

Palatine is in northwest Cook County. After the long agricultural era
(c. 1835–1950), the village grew rapidly beginning in the 1970s as part of
Chicago's suburban expansion, reaching a population of 67,908 by the 2020 census.

## Related landmarks and institutions

- **George Clayson House Museum** (1873) — National Register of Historic Places.
- **Deer Grove** — site of the earliest settlement; today a large forest
  preserve.
- **Palatine (CNW/Metra) station** — historic downtown railroad depot.
