# Palatine, Illinois — Timeline of Key Events

**Source:** Compiled from public reference material via web search.

- **1835** — George Ela builds a log cabin at Deer Grove; considered the first
  European-American settler in the area. First settlers arrive in the region.
- **1850** — Palatine Township is organized and adopts the name "Palatine."
- **1853** — The Illinois and Wisconsin Railroad reaches the Deer Grove Trading
  Post area.
- **1855** — Joel Wood surveys and lays out the Village of Palatine. Rail
  service reaches Palatine on June 10, 1855. The depot is built c. 1855 by
  Hiram Thurston on land donated by Joel Wood.
- **1866** — The Village of Palatine is incorporated on April 2, 1866.
- **1867** — George Clayson purchases ten acres of land from Palmer Webster for
  use as a nursery (fruit trees and grapes).
- **1868** — A new two-story school is built on Wood Street.
- **1869** — A substantial Lutheran church is built. The Village is chartered by
  the State of Illinois.
- **1871** — Street lights installed; the Village buys 12 lamps at $3 each and
  hires a lamplighter for 50 cents a night.
- **1873** — George Clayson builds his residence (the Clayson House).
- **c. 1835–1950** — The "great Age of Agriculture" in Palatine Township.
- **1955** — The Palatine Historical Society is organized in December 1955.
- **1970s** — Rapid suburban growth begins as part of Chicago's expansion.
- **1976** — The Palatine Public Library buys the Clayson House property in
  recognition of the American Bicentennial, with the Historical Society and
  Park District as co-trustees.
- **1979** — The Clayson House Museum opens in October.
- **1976–1986** — The Society raises $200,000 to restore the Clayson House.
- **1992–1993** — Village seal controversy: lawsuits by activist Rob Sherman
  over the Christian cross on the village seal; the Village ultimately drops
  the seal.
- **January 8, 1993** — The Brown's Chicken massacre occurs in Palatine.
- **2002** — A suspect in the Brown's Chicken case is implicated; DNA evidence
  is matched.
- **2007** — Juan Luna is convicted of seven counts of first-degree murder and
  sentenced to life imprisonment.
- **2020** — U.S. Census records a population of 67,908.
