## Who they are

The **Palatine Historical Society** is dedicated to the collection,
preservation, and exhibition of local history and memorabilia. The Society was
**organized in December 1955**.

## Mission

The organization's purpose is the collection, preservation, and display of
artifacts, and the discovery and dissemination of knowledge about the history of
Palatine and Palatine Township.

## Membership and the newsletter

Members receive the newsletter, **The Palatine Palaver**, mailed to their home,
which takes readers through the history of Palatine with stories drawn from news
articles and oral interviews with current and past members.

The Society maintains a **library at the Clayson House Museum** with many books,
maps, and pictures of Palatine. Members are welcome to explore the collection.
